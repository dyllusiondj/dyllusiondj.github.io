Privacy Policy: DYLLUSION Intelligence

Last updated: 20 October 2026

What this tool is

DYLLUSION Intelligence is a private analytics tool used only by the artist DYLLUSION to review the performance of his own content on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

  • Profile information and statistics of the DYLLUSION accounts (for example follower counts).
  • Public videos and posts of the DYLLUSION accounts and their metrics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Publicly available metadata of public professional accounts saved as reference content (captions and public counts).

How data is used

Data is used solely to generate internal performance reports and content recommendations for DYLLUSION. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a private repository and private workspace controlled by DYLLUSION. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and never published.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through the app permission settings of Instagram/Facebook, TikTok or Google. On request, all stored data is deleted.
